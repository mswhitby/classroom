# Data summaries and probability

**Strand:** statistics reasoning  
**Suggested duration:** 30 minutes  
**Status:** New instructional practice, not an official TSIA2 item or score predictor.

## Learn — guided worked example

**Problem:** For 2, 4, 4, 10, find the mean and median.

1. Mean: (2 + 4 + 4 + 10) ÷ 4 = 5.
2. Median: middle two values are 4 and 4, so (4 + 4) ÷ 2 = 4.
3. The mean is affected more by the high value 10.

**Think about it:** Which quantity or operation tells you that the answer is reasonable? Explain in one sentence.

## Practice — choose one answer

Work each problem before checking the explanations. The four choices are independent practice, not an official adaptive test.

- **A.** 5
- **B.** 6
- **C.** 7

### 2. Find the median of 12, 3, 8, 5, 9.

- **A.** 5
- **B.** 8
- **C.** 9
- **D.** 12

### 3. A bag contains 4 blue, 3 green, and 1 red marble. What is P(red)?

- **A.** 1/8
- **B.** 1/4
- **C.** 1/3
- **D.** 1/2

### 4. A fair six-sided die is rolled. What is P(even)?

- **A.** 1/6
- **B.** 1/3
- **C.** 1/2
- **D.** 2/3
