# Unit rates and proportions

**Strand:** quantitative reasoning  
**Suggested duration:** 30 minutes  
**Status:** New instructional practice, not an official TSIA2 item or score predictor.

## Learn — guided worked example

**Problem:** A recipe makes 6 servings with 9 cups of broth. How many cups for 10 servings?

1. Find the unit rate: 9 ÷ 6 = 1.5 cups per serving.
2. Multiply by 10 servings: 1.5 × 10 = 15 cups.
3. Check the units: cups/serving × servings = cups.

**Think about it:** Which quantity or operation tells you that the answer is reasonable? Explain in one sentence.
