# Week 1 facilitator overview

**Audience:** seniors who have not yet met the TSIA2 Math benchmark. **Facilitators:** math teachers. **Length:** five 30-minute advisory meetings, adaptable across calendar weeks.

## Purpose

Build confidence with quantities, proportional reasoning, equations, and linear relationships. The selected focus is narrow enough to teach and revisit within a week. These sessions do not cover the whole assessment, and a strong weekly check is not evidence of official TSIA2 completion.

| Day | Observable goal | Independent evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Distinguish a percent amount, a final amount, and percent change | Two exit items |
| 2 | Solve an equation and verify it by substitution | Two exit items |
| 3 | Find and interpret slope and an initial value | Two exit items |
| 4 | Compare two linear cost models using equal quantities | Two exit items |
| 5 | Apply the week's ideas independently | 10-item mixed check |

## Daily routine

Provide scrap paper or the printed packet. Use retrieval privately before a class discussion. Model the lesson examples, then work the two guided questions with students. During the four practice problems, ask students to show a setup before allowing calculator checks. Keep exit tickets independent: no hints or worked examples until first attempts have been collected. If a student needs support, record it and still provide the support.

Read all context and units aloud as needed; support access without telling students which operation to perform on the independent evidence. Use established accommodations. Avoid public rankings or labeling students by their locator code.

## Local instructional rules

These are planning rules, not validated mastery thresholds or TSIA2 cut scores:

- Daily exit 0/2: reteach with a concrete example, then try a new parallel problem.
- Daily exit 1/2: conference on the missed skill and check again on another day.
- Daily exit 2/2: continue, with a later retrieval item to check retention.
- Weekly check: examine errors by skill rather than using one total to assign readiness.

The tracking template has no student data. Store completed copies in a school-approved location outside GitHub. Use an existing school identifier locally instead of public student names.

## After the pilot

Note actual completion time, confusing wording, item-level mistakes, and teacher adaptations. Do not infer learning growth by comparing the two daily retrieval questions with the larger weekly check: those are not equivalent forms. Repeat selected skills on a later parallel check before treating them as retained.
