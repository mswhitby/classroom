# AI Usage Policy

Some of you have used AI to generate journal entries this year without labeling it. That stops now.

## What you need to do:
- Go back through your past journal entries.
- If you used AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, Grammarly AI, etc.), you must clearly label it:
  - Add: **“I used AI to assist with this entry.”**
- You must add a label to each journal that you used AI for assistance. 

## What will happen if you don’t:
- Any entry identified as AI-generated that is **not labeled** will be changed to a **0 in the gradebook**.

## Important distinction:
- Tools like Grammarly that help improve your writing are **allowed**.
- Submitting writing that was generated for you (by AI or copied from another source) is **not allowed**.

## Going forward (starting now):
- Any AI-generated journal submission = **automatic 0**
- **No resubmissions**

You are allowed to use AI as a tool—but not as a replacement for your thinking.
